# Competitive Analysis — Estudio

> **Upstream reference:** This analysis is grounded in the problem and ICP defined in `ideation/intent-capture/intent-statement.md`. [Q1-intent]

## Core Differentiation Message

> **"The only app that measures whether your mistakes are a real pattern or an isolated comment."** [Q2]

This message is positioned against the market's baseline: AI art critique is free and commoditized. Competing on critique quality is a losing position. Estudio competes on structured progress memory over time.

## Competitor Comparison

| Competitor | What they offer today | Gap vs. Estudio | Threat level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coartist** | Critique on 5 pillars, daily consistency tracking, activity calendars, score comparison over time | Does not measure trend by specific diagnostic category — the gap is narrow but real | **HIGH** — closest competitor; actively converging |
| **ArtKritic** | Instant critique, some portfolio/tone options, ~$4.99/month tier to remove ads | No trend tracking over time; pricing comparison not apples-to-apples (their paid tier removes ads, not unlocks progress) | Medium |
| **ArtCritic** | Instant critique | No trend tracking, no progress memory | Low |
| **Rate My Art AI** | Instant critique | No trend tracking, no progress memory | Low |
| **ChatGPT / Claude (generic prompt)** | Free instant critique via prompt | No persistent memory between sessions; no categorization structure | Medium — table stakes threat, not a progress-tracking tool |
| **draw it mcp (MCP connector)** | Free composition/technique analysis as an add-on to other tools | No structured accumulation over time | Low |
| **Drawabox (official paid critique)** | Human critique, $5–$10/lesson (subsidized below cost) | Human capacity bottleneck; does not connect critiques over time | Not a direct competitor — Drawabox critique is the complement Estudio supports |
| **Professional video critique (e.g. Aaron Rutten)** | $250/piece human critique | Illustrates market's price range for quality feedback; not a scalable product | Not a direct competitor |

Estudio and Coartist are both in the upper quadrant. The vertical axis gap between them is Estudio's current advantage. [Q1] [Q1-intent]

## What Estudio DOES NOT Compete On

- Quality of any individual critique (commoditized — free tools already do this)
- Speed of critique delivery
- Community features or social sharing
- AI art generation

Positioning Estudio on any of these is a losing strategy. [Q1-intent]
